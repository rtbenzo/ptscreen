PT Screen is an independent training log for a physical gate. It is not a Department of Defense score, and a result in the app does not ship you.

  • Pick the branch and the gate. Jump to the week you are already on.
  • Log reps, time, weight, points, or pass or fail, and add a note.
  • Attach a picture or video of your own set if you want one.
  • On a moving event, start a route. Distance and pace stay on the iPhone until you export them.
  • Allow location if you want the weather icon and temperature on Today.
  • Mark the day from 1 to 5, physical and emotional.
  • Email the last 28 days as a PDF, CSV, and Excel file from the share sheet, or from Mail when Mail is on the phone.

Confirm the current standard with your recruiter before you ship. Age and sex charts still decide the regular service tests. Selection courses want more than the minimum.
