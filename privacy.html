PT Screen · NetCore LLC

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

PT Screen is a personal training log published by NetCore LLC. It keeps an eight-week plan and the numbers you enter for the physical gate you pick. It is not a Department of Defense application, and it does not upload your log to NetCore LLC.

Who this applies to

The iOS app PT Screen (bundle ID com.netcore.PTScreen). There is no account and no cloud log.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The branch and gate you pick, and the week you jump to
  • Reps, times, weight, points, pass or fail, and your notes
  • Two daily marks, physical and emotional, each from 1 to 5
  • Photos and videos you attach to a log
  • A route, distance, and pace when you start tracking a moving event

NetCore LLC does not receive that information. There is no PT Screen server and no account.

Location

The app asks for location while you are using it.

  • The Today heading sends your coordinates to Open-Meteo to load a weather icon and temperature. That request is not tied to your name. NetCore LLC does not receive the coordinates or the forecast.
  • If you start a route on a run, ruck, swim, or other moving event, the path stays on the iPhone until you export it. Tracking can continue in the background only while that route is running, and iOS shows its location indicator. Stopping the route stops the updates.

Camera, microphone, and photos

The camera and microphone are used only when you record a picture or video of your own training. The photo library is used only when you attach a picture or video you already took. Those files stay on the iPhone.

Email and export

You may email or share a PDF, CSV, and Excel file when you choose. iOS cannot send that message silently. You confirm Mail or the share sheet. After you send, Apple Mail and your email provider handle delivery. NetCore LLC does not receive a copy.

What is not collected

No name, email, account, contacts, advertising identifier, or analytics. No tracking across apps or websites. No ads. No sale of data.

iCloud

The app does not write your log to iCloud as a cloud service. A device backup may include the app container. That backup belongs to your Apple ID, not to NetCore LLC.

Your controls

  • Deny location and the weather line stays blank. Routes will not record.
  • Deny camera, microphone, or photo access and those attachments stay unavailable.
  • Delete the app to remove the local log, photos, videos, and routes.

Children

Not directed at children under 13.

Not an official score

PT Screen is an independent training log. A result in the app is not a medical evaluation and not an official service score.
